# Real-flight trigger check

Generated by `tests/real-flight-triggers.test.mjs` (about 40 s, seeded) using `scripts/balance/real-flight-check.mjs`: a real card run (`createRun`), a real `WispFlight` on a landform course (row 2), flown by the repository pilot (`human150`). "Occurred" is counted independently of the cards (pickups collected, falling Lifts, Pulses emitted, deliveries). Assists, where a pilot cannot cause the trigger: steer to the Flare, metronome Lifts for Rhythm, a floor crash for Save, a recovering Collector for recovery cards. Counts are totals over 3 flights (6 where noted) and move when the course files change; the tests assert relations, not these numbers.

Resonant Harvest verdict: works. Pulses are emitted (4 per Flare), crossings are detected, the card fires once per crossing and pays 1 / 2 / 2 + 1 Stored. It looks dead because nothing but the one Flare per flight sends a Pulse in the owner's Chain (Lift Guide and Lantern Lens send none), and the pilot or Collector usually takes the Sparks before a Pulse reaches them (about 1 crossing per Flare). Same behaviour at commit 106a4e34. Rhythm (3 even Lifts) or Proximity feed it more.

## Not verified
- Crate Lining: no Cache was ever delivered by the Collector in 6 flights.
- Dream replay: attached by the app (`attachDream`), not reachable by the pilot harness (take-off eating is tested).
- Rest effects (Camp Kit, Growth Seed): model-level, not part of a flight.
- Skim: no released card has a Skim Trigger (Proximity is a Passive Pulse sender).
- Hidden cards (wave-loom ... whetstone): a 2-flight smoke run threw no errors, but engine cards pay through `cardEngine`, not the Chain receipt, so this harness cannot attribute their payouts. Not judged.

## Chain preview and Inscriptions
The Chain-tab preview ignores every Inscription: `dist/ui/chain-model.mjs:69` calls `previewCardRow({cards: row}, ...)` with no `inscriptions` (same for `card-row-preview.mjs:139,143`, `card-status.mjs:90`, `deck-combos.mjs:73-171`). Real flights: Rhythm, Echo, Body Spark+Gilded pays 8 per Collect; Rhythm(Twin Mark), Echo, empty, Body Spark+Gilded pays 4. Preview shows +4 and +2, so the +2 vs +4 difference in the captures is Echo losing its right-hand neighbour (the empty slot), not Gilded. Fix: pass `{cards: row, inscriptions: run.slotInscriptions}`.
