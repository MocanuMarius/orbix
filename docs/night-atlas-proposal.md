# Proposal: the Night Atlas

Status: the Map 2 experiment below is implemented. Numbered Ascension and existing companion unlocks remain in place; the larger progression changes are proposals.

Preserve the three-map expedition, the three guardian victories, selectable difficulty and the reason to start another run. Replace the numbered climb with a woodland atlas that remembers the kinds of nights the player has survived. Its main question is: which changes to the forest suit this expedition?

## The run

The first expedition stays as it is. After its final guardian the first road on the home illustration lights up and Protector unlocks. On a subsequent expedition the first guardian offers two illustrated forecasts for Map 2. The player sees each rule, its upside and the affected route branches before committing. The second guardian offers another forecast for Map 3. At ordinary difficulty the new forecast replaces the old one; there is never more than one active. A forecast remains fixed if the player loses a heart and takes another branch. All gestures remain Lift or the existing companion ability.

Sample forecasts, as design hypotheses:
- **Bellwind**: marked chimes sound before a local updraft carries the wisp to a high Spark shelf. Ride it and save a Lift, or pass below and stay on the ordinary fuel line. Runner's resistance changes the timing, but a body pickup and ordinary Lift must work for every companion.
- **Hollow Moon**: marked Black Holes slowly eclipse nearby sections. At peak eclipse the outer bypass briefly lights up, exposing a line of existing Sparks. Take the revealed route, wait for the phase, or take a safer connected branch. Core drain stays bounded and the outer lane must remain viable.
- Later candidates: Flooded Roots and Falling Stars. Do not add them unless we can describe a genuinely different steering decision and communicate it with one cue. A darker screen, harder drain, or differently coloured terrain alone does not qualify.

Forecasts move or reveal existing fuel; the opening, the regular clear payout and skill prices stay intact. Standard side bonuses are optional local tasks. Forecasts describe how the world behaves, never an extra assignment on the HUD or a mandatory bonus target. A full expedition always counts as a win. Winning under a new forecast records its illustration once; repeat nights remain playable. Unlock companions from a small number of distinct forecast clears, not a perfect maneuver or grinding duplicates. Existing unlocks stand when transitioning. No new currency. Normal and unmodified expeditions stay selectable.

## Challenge for repeat players

After establishing the individual forecasts, offer named rare nights that combine two familiar phenomena for the final map. These are authored pairings with their own guardian variant; they are not an arbitrary checklist of penalties. An example is a bell sounding *before* an eclipse: lift on the cue toward a high shelf before the outer bypass goes dark, or fly below. Players see the forecast pair before choosing to enter the harder voyage. Keep at least one playable ordinary branch and one more demanding way to exploit the conditions. Changing the illustrated destination is enough selection UI; keep the three home landmarks as read-only progress.

## First experiment

Build one between-guardian forecast choice in Map 2, two authored forks, and a visible response from its guardian. No starting menu, new currencies, unlock grind or stacked forecasts for this experiment. Try both forecasts on the same layout. Observe whether new players can predict what the chime/shadow means, whether the forecast actually changes which branch, gift or upgrade they choose, and whether they want to replay to try the other. If both choices play the same, or players simply pick the least punishing, change their benefits before authoring more.

Compare the same seeded roads without and with each forecast across each companion and starting gift. Keep the longest fuel drought, Energy at the first Rest and loss cause visible, especially with 150–350 ms input delay and limited vision. Never gate a companion behind an optional precision objective. The current proxy banked only six side bonuses in 120 flights. That is a warning about using those conditions as the price of progression, not a human completion rate. Confirm with actual phone play before setting thresholds.

Design grounding: [Ryan, Rigby & Przybylski (2006)](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf) associate autonomy and competence with enjoyment.
