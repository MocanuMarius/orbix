# New card proposals (`v1.0-cards`)

Written by the coordinator on 17 November 2026 at the owner's request: "design new cards and introduce interesting mechanics… a table of card proposals of different types… to either replace old ones that are boring… or simply introduce them." Nothing here is built. Numbers are first guesses for the simulation.

Design rules used (the owner's, from this project): plain card text; one Trigger and one effect per card; strength should depend on the build or the situation, not be good everywhere; no arbitrary drawbacks, no caps; broken combos are welcome; nothing to read while flying. Kept rules: Spark Meter 100; Boosts never multiply Stored Sparks or permanent gains; Sparks round down per firing.

What the numbers say is missing (`docs/card-balance-2.md`): every Trigger is won by the same Boost core (Echo III, Prism Lens II, Closing Chord, Crescendo, Refrain), so builds differ only in their first card; Flash ends, Rhythm, Save, Latch and Overfill have almost no cards; everything past 100 Sparks is wasted; nothing cares WHERE a card sits except "last" (Closing Chord) and "next" (Echo).

## 9. Oaths and curses (the owner's earlier idea: "more like the Closed Eye")

Oaths are bargains offered at an event stop: the player reads both sides and chooses. They are not random punishments. Curses come only from a choice the player made (a tempting chest, a shortcut).

| Card | Type | Text | Why it is fun |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Oath of Silence** | Oath | No Pulses for you. Collect cards fire twice. | Closes one build, doubles another |
| **Oath of the Low Road** | Oath | Max Sparks −10. Lift costs 1 less. | Changes how you fly, not just the numbers |
| **Oath of Hunger** | Oath | Your last Slot is sealed. Every other card: +2. | A smaller, stronger Chain |
| **Borrowed Light** | Curse (chosen) | Take a Legendary now. Lose 1 max heart until the next Rest. | The classic tempting deal |
| **Heavy Purse** | Curse (chosen) | +60 Stored Sparks now. Collector is slower this map. | Short pain, clear price |

Curses take a Slot in the Deck (not the Chain) until lifted at a Rest; each shows its end condition on the card.

## 11. Keyword: Devour (the owner's design, 17 November 2026)

The owner's words: "Some cards, like the Dream card, should have a devour mechanic where they destroy a card to the right of a certain rarity, and they get permanently upgraded up to a cap, like 40 seconds maybe. Destroying a Common should be +0.5 s, Rare +1 s and Legendary +3 s… maybe this mechanic needs a special keyword 'Devour', to be reused with Black Hole and so on."

**Devour** (keyword, with a glossary entry and its own icon): at take-off, this card destroys the card in the Slot to its right and grows. The growth is stored on the card, shown on its face, and leaves with the card (rule: a card's effect lives on the card). Nothing to its right: nothing happens. Never in practice; never a loaned card. The Chain screen marks the victim "Devoured at take-off" and asks once before the flight.

| Card | Devour gives (Common / Rare / Legendary eaten) | Cap | Face shows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dream** | Dream time +0.5 s / +1 s / +3 s | 40 s | "Devoured 5 · Dream 14.5 s" |
| **Black Hole** | Max Sparks +1 / +2 / +3 | meter cap 100 | "Devoured 4 · +7 max Sparks" |
| **Devourer** (rework) | to settle: hearts | to settle | — |
| **Ember Feeder** (rework) | to settle: a bigger meter now | to settle | — |

To settle when building: which rarities each Devour card may eat ("of a certain rarity": for example Dream eats any rarity, or only Commons unless improved); whether one Devour card can eat another; Dream's base time today and what a 40 s Dream means for the run; whether Devourer and Ember Feeder become plain Devour cards with different rewards or one of them retires.
