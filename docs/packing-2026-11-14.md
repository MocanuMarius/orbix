# Packing

Packing is the named conversion of surplus pickup Energy into Packed Sparks. Baseline: 6 Energy -> 1 Packed Spark. Efficient Packing adds 1/6 efficiency, as does Prism Vessel: either gives 6 -> 2; together 6 -> 3. Existing advanced skills add smaller improvements. Total efficiency remains capped at 100%. Fractions persist; rest healing does not mint currency.

The left meter animates two motes compressing into a pixel box. Completed packets rise to the upper-left wallet, followed by the actual credited amount. Incomplete packets show a fractional core, then fade locally without pretending a whole point was earned. The effect reuses DOM nodes and supports reduced motion.

3,456 modeled campaigns, 12 seeds across tiers 1/3/6: steady-policy median first purchase at flight 2; median 4 purchases over 14 flights; median per-run purchase rate .375. Practiced rate .375, learning .267. Thus pacing is slower than the earlier one-rank-per-two-flights target, particularly for learning players. Clear rewards remain unchanged to protect baseline progression; review after phone playtesting before adding more Packing relics. Raw results: work/economy-packing-sixth.json. These are policy simulations, not human win-rate estimates.
