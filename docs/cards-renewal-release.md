The safeguard preserves rarity rolls, saved receipts and starters, and Melt cannot restart it. Growth Ring now adds permanent maximum Sparks on every equipped flight clear without refilling. Tight Packing pays +50/75/100% of naturally packed currency by tier, with persistent fractional progress and no compounding of bonuses. Clear receipts settle once across reload and repeated callbacks.

Devourer consumes only the Rare card immediately to its right in the current equipped arrangement after a successful clear with a missing heart, then heals one. Full hearts, failed flights and Dream preserve the pair. Paused Deck edits change the next clear’s arrangement. Devourers resolve left to right after Heart Stitch, cannot copy or replay sacrifices, and preserve their own instances. Trail Vessel becomes Growth Ring and Bellows Seal becomes Wind Feather. Every migrated instance retains its identity; Bellows Seal’s former consumable count is removed because Wind Feather has no uses.
