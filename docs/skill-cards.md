# Skills as cards

**Proposal · 15 November 2026.** This supersedes the two-milestone, two-pick suggestion. The campaign and First Light stay unchanged; these are hypotheses for an isolated trial.

## The player loop

Start with **three ordered active slots**. Skills come from mounted cards. Each first real flight clear, including elites and guardians, drops **one card**. Choose **Equip** or **Set aside for melting**. Mount and reorder only at checkpoints; flight stays one-handed.

When full, choose the replacement deliberately. Preview the lost ability and the displaced card entering a compact **name · rarity · count** melting tally. Exhausted cards are discarded, not recycled. Avoid a large spare-card inventory.

For example, take a direct body Spark worth 12, with meter room and no packing conversion:

- **Echo → Body Spark +2 → Field Impulse:** 12 base +2 action +2 repeat = **16 to the meter**.
- **Body Spark +2 → Echo → Field Impulse:** 12 base +2 action = **14**. Echo cannot repeat Field Impulse.

“Body Spark +2” is illustrative. Field Impulse keeps its normal capability in either order. **Move left / Move right** previews totals, charges and compatibility: “Repeats +2” or “Cannot repeat Field Impulse.” At the right edge, Echo and Chameleon have no target.

Three slots means capacity, not three free cards. Could the existing starter gift supply the first Common without another drafting screen? Cards replace buying the same effects in the skill book for this trial.

## Card meaning and rewards

Keep these distinctions visible:

- **Rarity:** Common grey, Rare green, Legendary purple, with written labels.
- **Cost:** free to mount; optional improvements tentatively cost existing **Stored Sparks**, never flight fuel. No new currency.
- **Rank:** improvement within a card. Map level may unlock selected upgrades without automatically raising rarity. Tap to preview the actual number changed and price.
- **Charges:** remaining uses on that real card.
- **Temporary modifiers:** their own printed duration, separate from rank and charges.

Common is the default drop. Fight toughness proposes the value/cost budget and weights rarity; tougher fights improve odds rather than guarantee Legendary. Budgets, prices and odds remain open.

Keep elite/guardian relics separate and passive for comparison, budgeting card and relic rewards together rather than silently adding free value. **What useful reward belongs after the final guardian, when the run is over?**

## Copy, repeat and lifetime

Run-long means **while mounted**; limited-use cards persist only while charges remain. Recalculate passives on mounting, removal and reordering, without refilling the meter or granting a one-time effect twice.

**Field Impulse and fixed wards cannot be copied or doubled.** A compatible printed numeric payout, such as direct body pickup **+2 to the meter**, may be amplified or repeated. Its base pickup value stays unchanged.

**Echo** repeats its immediate right neighbour's compatible printed action when its natural event fires. **Chameleon** borrows the compatible printed ability immediately right. Neither recursively copies or repeats modifiers such as Echo or Chameleon.

Chameleon may borrow an explicitly copyable passive: **+3 maximum Sparks while mounted** becomes +6 capacity with the original. Echo has no activation to repeat. Reordering changes capacity without refilling fuel; run-long duration alone does not make an ability copyable.

Limited uses are shared with the real source **per execution**. Repeating +2 twice spends two uses; permitted numeric doubling gives +4 for one use. A borrowed execution also spends a source use. No remaining use means no repeat. Copies create no pickups, collection events or reward triggers. Swapping, reordering and fusion never refill spent charges or reset consumed effects.

## Melting

First trial: **three exact copies at one rarity → one seeded random card of the next rarity**, Common → Rare → Legendary. No tier above Legendary. Results survive reload and retry without rerolls.

Mounted ingredients count only when explicitly included. Preview their removal, empty slots and result placement; consume each once, never leaving a committed card active. Exhausted cards cannot melt.

Open questions: exact name or same printed family and rarity? Show the pre-rolled result or only its pool? How do partially spent ingredients constrain the result without refilling charges?

Supply matters: six independent Common drops from twelve equally likely designs have only an **11.4%** chance of any triple, even retaining every copy. This is an illustration, not campaign data. Small themed pools advertised on routes could make hunting intentional; compare the family recipe if exact matches rarely work.

## Rest and archetypes

At Rest choose **Recover, Grow or Add slot**: expansion costs the recovery or growth passed up. Proposed pilot: **five slots maximum, at most one addition per map**. Add slot restores neither hearts nor fuel; show Recover/Grow refills clearly. At cap, retain useful choices or Continue without resetting the run. Are slots worthwhile without becoming the guaranteed best camp choice?

Keep **Bankrunner** skimming and **Glider** coasting into a direct fuel Spark as archetype examples, each priming one free Lift. Candidate follow-ups reveal nearby space or accelerate a recovering Collector when that Lift is spent. Retain one stored free Lift, tentatively two priming triggers per flight, and preserve the opportunity while a free Lift is stored. Show each step clearly.

## What the prototype should answer

Compare with the skill book at **equal economy on the same seed**, across open-middle and riskier-bank routes. Require neither Proximity nor Dream or expensive granted skills. Prevent practice carryover, generated loops and Dream/retry farming.

Use a small card pool. Record actual flights and duplicate supply, achievable fusions, equips/set-asides, reordering, direct versus Collector pickups, bank time, routes, survival and camp choices. Ask why players changed flight line and whether they predicted combinations and charge costs. Do the choices add readable archetypes beyond the book, and can they fit comfortably on a phone?
