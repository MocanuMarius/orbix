# Archetype gaps and Flash (`v1.0-gaps`)

Written by a worker on 17 November 2026 for the coordinator. Nothing here is built. It answers the owner's request: "look into making some balance changes to create more cool combos and/or archetypes, maybe new cards, so we cover some spots that are not so rich in interactions, power or mechanics", and her Flash question: "Flash should be reworked into Pulse? The triangle is just too rare… needs better thinking."

Rules kept: no caps, nerfs or arbitrary drawbacks; crazy combos welcome; strength depends on build and context; a card's effect lives on the card; Spark Meter cap 100; Boosts never multiply Stored Sparks or permanent gains; Sparks round down per firing.

**Measured** means a script or the real game code produced it. **Estimated** means my reading of card text or my judgement. Each section says which.

Sources: `dist/card-catalog.mjs`, `dist/card-trigger-labels.mjs`, `outputs/balance/cards-unreleased.md` (simulation, run today with `--include-unreleased`), `outputs/balance/engines.md`, `docs/card-balance-2.md`, `docs/card-proposals.md`, `dist/flight.mjs` (`buildFlightLayout`).

## 4. Flash

### How often the pickup appears

**Measured** two ways.

**From the code.** `buildFlightLayout` in `dist/flight.mjs` (around line 723) rolls once per flight: 58% for one Flash pickup ("sight-niche", Collect lane, between 32% and 58% of the way along). The first ordinary flight (row 0) never gets one. Other Flash sources are rare: a "passage-flash" in the hardest lantern room (`dist/lantern-layout.mjs`), a "crossfire-flash" in one guardian fight, and the route objective "flash-relay". Flash Tinder (unreleased) makes a 1 s Flash from a Collect. One pickup lights the scene for 8 s and pulses every 2 s.

**From a seeded sample of the real builder.** I built 10,080 real layouts (120 seeds per cell: rows 0 to 7, three length bands, normal and elite, all 63 levels, with the integer difficulty the game uses) and counted Flash pickups. No build threw an error. The old layouts (`landforms:false`, the `?courses=1` path) were sampled the same way.

| Slice | Flash pickups per flight (measured) |
| --- | --- |
| Row 0 (first flight), old and new | 0.00 |
| Normal, rows 1 to 7, **new landform courses** | 0.19 (about one in 5 flights) |
| Normal, rows 1 to 7, **old authored courses** | 0.56 (the 58% roll almost always lands) |
| Elite, rows 1 to 7, new / old | 0.21 / 0.56 |
| Short / Standard / Endurance, new, normal | 0.17 / 0.21 / 0.19 (elite: 0.17 / 0.30 / 0.17) |
| Short / Standard / Endurance, old | 0.56 / 0.56 / 0.56 |
| Landform conversion effect | Flash pickups are about 3 times rarer (0.19 against 0.56, a 66% drop) |

Length makes no clear difference (one roll per flight). The landform conversion made Flash rarer because the lane needs a wide, clear stretch and the landform courses leave fewer of them. The guardian and lantern extras did not show up in the sample at all.

**What it means.** A run has about 21 flights, so a run sees about 4 Flash pickups (about 11 under the old courses). Each gives two Flash events (pickup, then light-out). The simulation assumes 3 Flash firings and 3 "Flash ends" per 30 s. For a 95 s flight that is about 9 of each, against a real 0.2 and 0.2. **The Flash cards fire about 45 times less often than the balance simulation assumes.** Every Flash number in `cards-unreleased.md` is a fiction. Flash Handling's 81% share of the Flash chains means nothing for the real game.

### Option (a): Flash folds into Pulse

Taking the pickup sends one strong Pulse. The Flash trigger is removed (9 triggers become 8). The six cards become Pulse cards. Minimum **N = 1** per flight is enough, because Pulse cards also fire from Rhythm, Proximity and the rest.

| Card | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Flash Handling | Becomes a Pulse card: Pulse, +6. Keeps "Collector can fetch the pickup" |
| Clear Prism | Becomes "Pulse: double every reward before it". Nearly the same as Ripple Glass; one has to change or they merge |
| Flash Thread | "Pulse: Collector ready 3 s sooner". Pulse fires several times a flight, so this becomes much stronger and needs retuning (uses) |
| Flash Bank | "Pulse, meter ⅓ or less: +8". Works as is |
| Prism Wick, Afterglow | "When a Flash ends" has no Pulse home. Move to "when the pickup's light runs out", or merge into Pulse with a final Pulse |

- **Richer:** Pulse gets 5 more cards (6 live becomes 11). The six cards fire as often as any Pulse card, so every Flash number becomes real.
- **Lost:** The unique Flash identity; the pickup is a Pulse source and nothing more. The Flash-ends timing. Prism Wick is already a Flash-to-Pulse bridge, and it would lose that job.
- **Cards to reword:** 6, plus the glossary (Flash term, trigger chip, tutorial text, "Flash Powerup" onboarding card) and combo tables.
- **Risk:** Medium. Cheap to build. The danger is that Pulse is already crowded and Clear Prism and Ripple Glass collide. It does not fix the rarity of the pickup itself, only hides it, because Pulse fires often by other sources.

### Option (b): Flash stays a trigger, guaranteed at least N per flight, each worth more

- **Richer:** Keeps the white triangle as a thing to chase and a scouting tool. All six cards keep their words. Each Flash gives two events, pickup and light-out, so 2 pickups per flight is 4 events.
- **Lost:** Darkness. Each Flash lights the scene for 8 s. Two Flashes per flight lights about 16 s of a 95 s flight. At the simulation's 3 per 30 s, the game would be lit for 80% of the time, which kills the dark lanes and Dark Sparks. So N must stay low.
- **Cards to reword:** 0. Numbers change: at about 0.04 events per second with N = 2 (4 per 95 s; about 0.4 per 95 s today) against the simulation's 0.1, Flash numbers should be roughly 3 to 5 times higher to match peers (for example Flash Handling +6 becomes about +20, Flash Thread 3 s becomes 6 to 9 s). This is a buff, not a nerf.
- **Risk:** Low to medium. One layout rule: place N pickups spread along the flight (for example at one third and two thirds), and keep the existing roll as an extra. Changes the feel of the darkness design. Needs a pass on the layouts that have few safe lanes.

### Option (c): Flash becomes something the player can cause

For example a small Flash charge fills from Skims or Rhythm (every Skim fills a third; when full, a Flash starts at the wisp). The pickup stays as a bonus.

- **Richer:** Joins Skim (0 live cards) with Flash (6 cards). Proximity and Edge Reach finally have a purpose. Player skill creates the trigger, not luck. Flash Tinder (Collect makes a 1 s Flash) already works this way, so the design has a start.
- **Lost:** Flash stops being "a pickup you find". A new meter is a new thing to read in flight; it must be icons only, no text.
- **Cards to reword:** 0 to 2 (the glossary line for Flash; Flash Handling "Collector can fetch" stays).
- **Risk:** High. A new mechanic, new UI, and needs its own balance work. Easy to loop: Skim, Flash, Pulse, Skim. That is a crazy combo, which the owner welcomes, but the charge size must be tested.

### Recommendation

**Option (b) with N = 2 per ordinary flight (rows 1 and up), and 3 on Endurance and guardian flights. Ship Flash Tinder as the player-caused piece.** I would not do (a) because it solves the rarity by deleting the trigger and creates a Clear Prism and Ripple Glass collision. I would not start with (c) because it needs a new meter, but it is the right second step once Skim has payoffs.

Reason for N = 2. Each pickup lights 8 s and gives 2 events. Two per flight gives 4 events a flight, which matches the glossary line that Flash happens "a few times" (Delivery and Overfill carry the same label). It lights about one sixth of a standard flight, so darkness still matters. N = 3 on the long flights keeps the rate even, about one Flash per 40 s. N = 1 would give 2 events and the cards would still feel absent. N = 3 everywhere would light a quarter of a standard flight.

I recommend one more card from the existing proposals: **Storm Glass** (section 5). The Flash pulses every 2 s, so one pickup is 4 Pulses. A card that lets each of them fire your Pulse cards gives Flash a Pulse bridge without folding the trigger.

## 6. Five builds to aim for

Chain order is left to right. An asterisk marks an unreleased card. Marked paper builds: I checked the card text, not the engine, and did not simulate them.

1. **Collect into Delivery:** Dusk Wick II, Tributary\*, Courier Tip III, Courier Glass, Closing Chord. *Collect feeds Delivery every third Spark, the Glass doubles it and the Chord closes the Chain. Two builds in one.*
2. **Skim into Pulse:** Proximity, Close Call\*, Resonant Harvest (Legendary), Far Shore, Ripple Glass. *Hug the terrain, the Skim sends a Pulse, the Pulse cards pay out down the row. Flying close is the engine.*
3. **Overfill and the bigger meter:** Dusk Wick III, Overflow Bell\*, Spill Jar\*, Gilded Rim\*, Tight Packing III. *A big Dusk Spark spills on purpose; the spill becomes Stored Sparks and a bigger meter, and Tight Packing makes the Stored count.*
4. **Crash and comeback:** Watchlight, Home Beacon, Last Light, Second Wind\*, Scar Tissue\*. *The crash is the plan: Collector Saves you, the Chain fires on everything for 5 s, and Scar Tissue grows with every Save.*
5. **Player-made Flash:** Flash Tinder\*, Prism Wick, Afterglow\*, Clear Prism, Closing Chord. *Collect starts a short Flash; its end pays out twice. A Flash build that does not wait for the triangle.*

A sixth build (the Timers engine: Close Call, Metronome, Downbeat III from `engines.md`) works only once the engine cards ship.
