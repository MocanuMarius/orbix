Each service reviews its exact result and Stored Spark cost before confirmation, uses no Rest action, and remains available on the completed Rest preparation screen until choosing the onward route. Cancelling spends nothing. Existing Maps keep their saved route layout.

Twelve more Common cards now have finite, successful-effect uses. Previously unlimited saved instances receive their printed starting uses while keeping identity and rank; existing partial uses stay partial. Exhausted cards leave the Deck. Packing efficiency appears as +50%, +75% or +100%, with additive copies, concrete conversion examples and fractional rules in the glossary. Prism and Crescendo still multiply bonus flight Sparks; they do not multiply packing efficiency or Stored Sparks. The existing Overfill mechanic is unchanged.

Card frames share aligned artwork and rules areas across rarities while retaining the Eclipse design. Keywords support nested touch help, keyboard focus and desktop hover. The catalog now contains 82 cards. Dusk Sparks use their own cracked violet symbol and art; contact dims Luminance as before and now drains 3 Sparks before Dusk Wick pays its printed bonus. New Maps add readable Dusk encounters without replacing ordinary fuel approaches. The Common Lantern Lens adds 20% Luminance radius while equipped and appears in varied early card drafts.

Completed Games record a personal score by Ascension and overall on this device. Gameplay earnings remain counted after camp spending. A frozen scorecard can be saved as a PNG or shared by a stateless friend-comparison link; opening a shared card changes no save or personal best. Games begun before score tracking show an unranked partial score when their existing final settlement runs. Tutorial, test and abandoned Games do not set personal bests. The main menu and newly produced scorecards use the same release version.
