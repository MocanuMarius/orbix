# Inscriptions: effects written on Chain slots

Status: built. Artifacts are retired for players; Inscriptions replace them. Code: `dist/inscriptions.mjs` (catalogue and helpers), `dist/inscription-ui.mjs` (brand, etched frame, inspector block), brand marks `dist/inscription-glyphs.mjs` + `dist/assets/ui/inscriptions/<id>.svg` (drawn and generated by `scripts/build-inscription-glyphs.mjs`; captures: `scripts/inscription-ui-proof.mjs`), the Scribe in `dist/model.mjs` (`chooseFlightReward`, `previewScribeWrite`) and `dist/reward-screen.mjs` (`scribeMarkup`). Tests: `tests/inscriptions.test.mjs`.

## Slot rules

- An Inscription is written on a Chain **slot** (by index), at most one per slot: `run.slotInscriptions`, aligned with `run.mountedCards` (`null` or `{id}`). It grows with the Chain (a new slot gets a bare entry) and is saved with the run.
- Two kinds. **Card-modifying** changes the card sitting in that slot and does nothing while the slot is empty. **Standalone** works while it is on any slot, even an empty one.
- It stays on the slot when cards are moved, swapped, melted, sold, devoured or taken out. The card that leaves loses the effect; the next card placed there gets it.
- Repeat and Boost cards treat a card as it behaves in its slot: Echo repeats the modified firing, and a Boost multiplies Sparks an Inscription adds. Boosts never multiply Stored Sparks or permanent gains.
- Writing on an inscribed slot replaces it: the writer screen shows the old one struck through on that slot before Write (no separate confirm).
- Global rules hold: Spark Meter cap 100, Sparks round up per firing, events flow right, only Timers return to the first slot. Nothing is readable in flight: the slot brand, the inspector and the Scribe are menu screens only (Bell sends a real light Pulse, a non-reading cue).
- No charges: none of the 18 needs a stored count. Whetted gives its extra use inside the flight only, so it is free and never kept.

## How players get them: the Scribe

**Marks.** Every Inscription has its own brand mark on a **24×24** grid in two tones of the brand colour: ink, and an engraved second tone (carved inner lines, highlights, grain) at half strength (`fill-opacity .5`, CSS class `ins-glyph-cut`). Drawings: `scripts/build-inscription-glyphs.mjs` (ASCII, `#` ink, `+` second tone); generated `dist/inscription-glyphs.mjs` and `dist/assets/ui/inscriptions/<id>.svg`. Shown only at whole multiples, one size per role (`INSCRIPTION_GLYPH_SIZES`): **list 24** (inspector, glossary list, slot lines, briefing), **large 48** (offers on the writer; 24 on its compact short-landscape slots), **huge 72**, **brand 26** (burned into the slot frame's top-left corner, 1-pixel lighter edge, grid 26) and **brandLarge 52** (an empty inscribed slot; 26 on narrow slots). The CSS role rules live in `dist/artifact-reward.css` ("One size per glyph role") and win over generic icon rules. On narrow Chain slots the eye button moves right so it never covers the brand; on an inscribed slot the NEW badge moves to the right of the eye. Audit and screenshots: `node scripts/glyph-audit.mjs` → `outputs/glyph-audit/`, findings in `docs/glyph-audit.md`. Contact sheet: `node scripts/inscription-ui-proof.mjs sheet` → `outputs/inscriptions/glyphs.png`.

**Tags.** Choice screens show a small tag instead of the long kind sentence: **Card** (changes the card in its slot) or **Slot** (works on its own, even on an empty slot), each a glossary link (`ins-card`, `ins-slot` in `dist/card-terms.mjs`).

**The writer (one screen).** A map's Guardian reward and the Scribe stops (and a chest's Inscription find) open ONE screen (`dist/inscription-write.mjs`): the offers in the top row (**2** from the Scribe, `SCRIBE_OFFER_COUNT`; 1 from a chest, already selected), the Chain as it is in the bottom row, every slot visible (3 to 6, shrinking evenly; nothing scrolls at 890×400, 915×412, 740×333). Tap an offer and a slot in either order; nothing is preselected. The chosen slot shows the result before anything is written: the new brand, the old Inscription struck through ("Replaces Gilded"), and the card's before → after from the Chain tab's own row preview (`resolveChain` with the slot Inscriptions), e.g. "+2 → +4 on Collect"; a Card Inscription on an empty slot says "Needs a card here". The single primary button **Write** (Replace when it replaces) is disabled until both are chosen. The "?" on an offer, a card or a brand and every keyword open the glossary as an overlay; the selection stays. Offers never include an Inscription already written on a slot unless fewer than two others remain. Practice (First Light) uses the same screen through `practiceScribeWrite`.

### Two Scribes per map, and the map layout (`rests-scribes-1`)

Every card-run map offers exactly **two** Scribes (a run: 6). Maps 1 and 2: one **Scribe stop** about the middle of the map plus the Guardian's reward. Map 3: **two Scribe stops**, at about a third and two thirds; its Guardian ends the run and awards none. A Scribe stop is a non-flight stop like Rest (node `kind: 'scribe'`, `type: 'support'`): entering it opens the Scribe at once (`ensureScribeStop` draws three Inscriptions from the node's seed, so re-entry shows the same three), you write one, and `leaveScribe` returns to the map. Backing out before writing (`cancelScribeStop`) does not count the visit. Stop page: "Scribe — choose an Inscription for a slot of your Chain." (reward line "1 Inscription (choose 1 of 2)"). Map icon: the Whetted brand from `inscriptionGlyph()` (a nib resting on a slab), no new art.

Layout guarantees (recipes `MID_ROWS` and `FINAL_ROWS` in `dist/model.mjs`): maps 1 and 2 have 15 rows (was 10), map 3 has 17. Every Rest and every Scribe stop sits **alone on its row**, and the row before it funnels every lane into it, so no path can skip one. Every path passes 3 Rests (rows 4, 10, 13 on maps 1-2; rows 4, 9, 15 on map 3), never in consecutive rows. A fourth Rest sits on a side lane of row 2: take it or skip it. Elites, treasures and events stay at 2 each; ordinary flights rise from 11 to 15 (map 3: 17) because rows were added instead of flights removed. Supports are still entered from flights and leave into flights. Tutorial and legacy (non-card) maps are unchanged. Tests: `tests/map-layout.test.mjs`.

Old saves: a saved map keeps its nodes as generated. New-layout nodes carry `routeTopology: 'rests-scribes-1'`; old card maps carry `'longer-journeys-2'`, 10 rows and no Scribe stop (their Guardian still awards its Scribe). `validRun` accepts 10, 15 and 17 rows. Only maps generated after the update (the next map of an old run) use the new layout.

Dev: `?mock=cards&chain=...&slots=0:gilded,2:hollow` writes Inscriptions on a mock run (`applyInscriptionSlots` in `dist/card-inspector.mjs`).

## What happened to artifacts

- Not awarded: Elite rewards (they give their card only), the last map’s Guardian (it ends the expedition), anything past 6 Scribes in one run (`SCRIBE_MAX_PER_RUN` = 2 per map x 3 maps), chest relics (Chest finds keep only skills, hearts and Stored Sparks) and legacy starting gifts no longer grant them.
- Not applied: `getRunModifiers` ignores `run.artifacts`; a loaded run drops `run.artifacts` and `run.artifactCharges` (no conversion). Companion-tuning and perk-bound artifacts vanish with the rest.
- Run screens: the Rest "Collection" button is gone; the map collection dock only draws for a held artifact, so it no longer appears. Left alone: the meta Collection and Relic almanac pages, `ARTIFACTS` data, artifact art and copy modules.
- Tutorial (First Light): lessons are not rewritten and the tutorial awards no artifact. Texts that still mention artifacts, to reword next: `dist/tutorial-curriculum.mjs` (the rewards and artifacts topics, "A reward for every clear", "Flight and elite routes", "Three maps make a run"), `dist/onboarding.mjs` ("Choose one artifact"), `dist/map-guide.mjs` (elite and guardian stop lines), `dist/card-guide.mjs` (the relics line), `dist/artifact-lessons.mjs`.

### How they work

- **Entry.** `{id, temporary: true, flightsLeft: N | until: 'fulfilled', covers: {id} | null}` in `run.slotInscriptions[slot]`. The engine modifiers (`flightBoons`, `eventFavors`, pact) are read from the Chain (`dist/passage-preparations.mjs`), so the flight rules and numbers are unchanged.
- **Earning.** The award sets `run.pendingTemporary`; the router (`goToRun`) opens the slot screen, the same screen style as the Scribe. Nothing else can proceed (`selectNode`, `beginFlightAttempt`, `resolveSupport`, `acknowledgeVictory` refuse) until it is written. Tests and scripts may call `writePendingTemporary(run)` without a slot (first bare slot).
- **Covering.** Over a permanent Inscription it covers it: the permanent one is suspended (no rule sees it) and comes back by itself when the temporary one ends. The screen asks first and says so. Over another temporary one it replaces it (confirm) and keeps whatever that one covered. A Scribe write under a temporary one lands beneath it. Earning the same temporary Inscription again moves it and refreshes its count; effects never stack.
- **Counting.** A cleared flight spends one flight of each that was on the Chain when the flight began; a fall or an interrupted flight spends none. At zero it ends. Lead Seal ends when the flight starts (an unstarted gate gives it back).
- **Oaths** stay until a cleared flight fulfils them (a fall leaves them in place; the Vow stays open with fewer than 4 body pickups, the Purse with fewer than 12 Sparks). This differs from the old "one attempt consumes it, falls included".
- **Display.** The brand uses the Inscription's glyph in a dashed ring (accent `#a4561f`) with a pixel counter of flights left at the top-right of the brand; an oath wears a small open ring. The inspector says "Temporary: N flights left" or "Oath: stays until fulfilled" on the dark Inscription panel, with the covered Inscription small and grey. Menus only.
- **Stop pages and offers** use one line, `temporaryLine(id)`: "Temporary Inscription: Mending — below 6 Sparks, restore up to 6 missing Sparks once per flight. 3 flights." Event stops list the Inscriptions they can give (`EVENT_SCENARIOS[i].gives`). Test: offer text, stop page and award are the same line.
- **Old saves.** `migrateLooseEffects` (run on load and when an attempt begins) moves loose effects onto the first slot without a temporary Inscription (covering if needed) with their remaining count; with no slot they are dropped. A run with no Chain (legacy non-card runs) drops them.
- Dev: `?scenario=temp-write&temp=mending` shows the slot screen; `slots=0:gilded,0:mending~2,1:closed-eye` writes them. Captures: `node scripts/inscription-ui-proof.mjs sheet temp`.

### Chests

A chest on a card run keeps its own two finds (some of which already give Stored Sparks; that base is unchanged) and then offers ONE more choice on its own screen, with two large options: **Stored Sparks: +N** (20 on map 1, 30 on map 2, 40 on map 3; no flight-clear scale exists for a support stop), taken at once, or **A surprise card**, a free Forge (Forge ceremony, skippable, one fade under reduced motion). The card goes to the deck (reserve) by the claim path won cards use (NEW badge, auto-mounted only when nothing is mounted), live pool, the ordinary stop reward odds, no Stored Sparks spent. One or the other, never both. The stop page says "Choose: +N Stored Sparks or 1 surprise card". Code: `queueChestChoice`, `chooseChestReward`, `grantChestCard` in `dist/model.mjs` (`run.pendingChest` blocks the map like `pendingTemporary`), `chestChoiceMarkup` in `dist/treasure-view.mjs`. Tests: `tests/chest-forge.test.mjs`.

### Order after a guardian: Scribe first, temporary second

A guardian clear on map 1 or 2 can owe both: the Scribe's permanent Inscription (choose 1 of 2 and a slot on one screen) and, when its optional objective was fulfilled, a temporary one (`run.pendingTemporary`). The router (`goToRun` in `dist/app.mjs`) shows the Scribe first and the temporary slot screen after it is written. Before 2026-11-18 the temporary screen came first and looked like the Scribe, and a Scribe pick written on the same slot afterwards sat beneath the temporary one (it only looked temporary). The Scribe screens are headed "The Scribe — permanent Inscription" and carry the note "A second choice follows" while one is owed; the temporary screen is headed "Temporary Inscription", states its source ("Objective reward: lasts 3 flights") and shows the chosen glyph in the dashed ring. A Scribe pick is always a plain `{id}` entry. Map 3's guardian owes neither. Tests: `tests/boss-chest-inscriptions.test.mjs`.

### Chest gifts: five more permanent Inscriptions

Far Lens (luminance reaches 15% farther), Quick Lens (companion recharge takes 15% less time), Mending Wick (recover 3 extra Sparks after each clear), Toll Stamp (each clear with 3 body pickups: +3 Stored Sparks), Lamplighter's Seal (each clear holding 12 Sparks: +3 Stored Sparks; the Sealed Purse oath still pays 24, or 36 with the Seal in force). Catalogue entries in `INSCRIPTIONS` flagged `chest: true` (standalone, own brand glyphs): they are NOT in `INSCRIPTION_IDS` (the Scribe's 18) and otherwise behave like any permanent Inscription (inspector, glossary, covering rule, replace-confirm). `grantTreasureGift` queues the write (`queueInscriptionWrite`, `run.pendingTemporary` with `permanent: true`, same slot screen, headed "Chest find — permanent Inscription"); the Seal is queued when the Sealed Purse is fulfilled. Effects read the Chain (`treasureRank` = written and not covered). Chest find text: "Inscription: <name> — <effect>" (`giftLine`). Old saves: `migrateChestGifts` moves the loose `treasureGifts` keys to the first slot with no permanent Inscription (none: slot 1, replacing; no slots: dropped); only `vessel` stays loose. NOTE: in normal runs a chest offers `chestRewardPair` (skills, hearts, Stored Sparks, glass seed), not `treasureChoices`; `treasureChoices` (where these finds are offered) is reached only by the tutorial, so the gifts are not currently obtainable in a normal run until they are added to `CHEST_REWARDS`.
