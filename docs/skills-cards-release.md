# Skills as cards release — 15 November 2026

Release marker: `skills-cards-1`. [Open the card pilot](https://mocanumarius.github.io/orbix/cards.html) · [Collector art comparison](https://mocanumarius.github.io/orbix/collector-art.html) · [Pilot rules and provisional economy](skill-cards.md).

## Shipped behavior

- `cards.html` is a fresh installed-phone entry. One tap finishes the release download and opens the normal New Game menu with `?cards=1`. Phone routing stays native; desktop routing retains the landscape host. Explicit `collectorArt`, `display` and `zoom` choices survive the launcher. After the complete download, the launcher and pilot work offline; an unprepared offline visit asks for a connection.
- The pilot has a separate save and starter seed. It starts with three ordered slots and one of two Common gifts. Each first real clear awards one card; Equip/replacement/Melt and checkpoint improvements preview the actual effects, charges and Stored Sparks transaction. The final guardian's card becomes a victory trophy. Standard play retains the skill book.
- Every pilot map offers two optional reachable Rest forks. Recover, Add slot and Forge share the normal one-action allowance. Mounted Growth Seed unlocks Grow; Second Ember spends its charge only on a successful second distinct eligible action. Forging previews the exact seeded card and costs both Stored Sparks and a Rest action. Add slot and Forge grant no healing or refill.
- Lift Guide is an optional Common starter and a temporary loan during card-mode First Light's Lift lesson. Rare Rhythm consumes each successful even trio of manual Lifts for one Luminance wave. Three Resonant Harvest rarities reward natural uncollected Spark crossings without collecting them or paying from generated objects. Echo and Chameleon use the real immediate-right source, including its shared finite charges. Field Impulse remains uncopyable.
- Common Tight Packing joins the 16-card pool as a fixed-rank mounted passive: “Pack 25% more Stored Sparks. Every 4 you pack earns 1 extra.” Actual overflow/exit packing earns the bonus, with integer quarter carry across successful clears and saves. Failed/Dream attempts cannot farm it; flat rewards, Melts, gifts, pact payouts and other unrelated grants retain their normal value. Chameleon can borrow the passive; Echo has no action to repeat. Card reward and final victory show the matching flight's named bonus receipt, with no stale/Forge receipt.
- Collector delivery/discard handling cues appear in both modes. The separate art gallery has matched full-app films, native crops and front/behind/fetch stills. Its two- and three-ink candidates are opt-in for that visit; the current four-tone Collector remains the default. Gallery play links pass through the update launcher.
- The Spirit Fire mineral rim's grain, hot patches and specks stay attached to world pixels. A narrower inward band makes it quieter. Rendering spends 4.5 reach units per unit of rock instead of 3, making penetration one third shallower while preserving the open-air law and dynamic physical shadows. [Matched actual-renderer frames](https://mocanumarius.github.io/orbix/assets/previews/terrain-light-comparison.png) compare the same seed, geometry, camera and source radius.
