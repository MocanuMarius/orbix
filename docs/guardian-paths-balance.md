# Guardian paths · 14 November 2026

| Question | Evidence | Design decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do giants actually stay rare? | 137 out of 13,768 natural Sparks across 300 seeded flights (0.995%); six were giant Dark Sparks. | Draw for a giant about once per hundred Sparks, then require sufficient space. Every sampled Dark giant had a body-wide bypass. |
| Does increased mass make bosses impossible? | In 35 matched delayed-reaction trials, original-size Sparks cleared 17 guardian fights; variable sizes cleared 16. Low-fuel defeats went from five to three. | Keep forgiving contact for guardian and opening fuel. Exclude large masses near guardian attacks and tight approaches. Treat this sample as directional. |
| Does a 2.5× stronger Black Hole still offer a clear alternative? | All 192 sampled ordinary-course / viewport combinations contained a continuous zero-drain lane. At ordinary speed a pass 100 world units from the core cost 9.3 displayed Sparks; beyond 220 the drain is zero. | Keep inverse-square falloff and the original field size. Relocate existing nearby fuel onto the checked bypass. No direct collision death at the core. |
| Does stronger drain matter in play? | In 36 matched ordinary flights, with limited vision and 150–350 ms reaction profiles, mean drain was 1.47 vs 3.52 Sparks. Whole-flight clears were 21 vs 19. Strong drain began only 0.22 s after median detection in the stronger version before improving its warning. | Improve the warning before changing fuel supply globally. Watch Ascension 2 in phone playtests. |
| How many skills fit in a run? | In 288 conditional full clears per curve, 0.12 to 0.24 training growth changed median purchases from 11 to 9, with the median first purchase still after flight one. The new curve produced 0.588 paid purchases per flight. | Keep small choices cheap. Scale later prices so the player has to commit to a build. |

The flight samples use simulated pilots with incomplete sight, delayed inputs and missed cues. The economy sample assumes completed flights and uses scenario estimates for pickups and misses. Neither is a measured player win rate or a physical-phone frame-time benchmark.
