# Economy, meaningful upgrades, relic tiers — 2026-11-13

This supersedes the older price and reward figures in top-three-economy-decisions.md.

Normal runs begin with 30 Embers and one free starting root. TEST bypasses unlocks without adding money or fuel; the explicit Grant test Embers action is separate. Ordinary skills cost mostly 80–110. Companion core paths retain three meaningful purchases; tiny three-part deep forks are now one whole-effect specialization. The catalog contains 104 visible node definitions, or 109 paid purchases when repeatable ranks are counted. All 109 purchases change an engine modifier or a model rule in focused validation.

The economy target is one meaningful purchase per roughly two cleared flights, beyond the free gift. Ordinary clear pay is 14 + 3 per map, with +3 for gauntlet routes and +12 for elites. Cash alternatives remain 18/21/24 ordinary, +10 for elites. Common/rare artifact resale remains 12/16, so buying a relic simply to sell it never beats taking cash. Boss resale is 24 and only concerns already-owned permanent boss relics. Final boss rewards have no unusable cash option. Event risks and optional challenge bonuses remain bounded.

Skill effects were strengthened with costs: Vitality lowers Lift effort 12%, Glowkeeping another 10%, Momentum adds 8% speed, Slipstream another 6%. Companion Spark nodes now give Collector +12% reach, Protector −2 internal Glow shield cost, Space Timer +0.35s hold, or Runner −0.75s recharge. Full deep forks apply their full tradeoff immediately, such as Rich Cargo's +4 internal Glow on deliveries. The engine still uses 100-unit precision while the UI shows 30 full Glow; descriptions are converted to whole display units.

The seeded income benchmark uses real generated pickup inventories, actual model purchases/routes/rewards, three imperfect pickup and error policies, and seven focus plans. It misses approximately 30–41% of beneficial packets; it does not use perfect targeting. Among the seven ordinary focus plans, every plan has a median of 8 paid nodes, generally 7–10 between the tenth and ninetieth percentiles, over 15–16 cleared flights. These are economic scenarios with assumed error rates, not measured human win probabilities. The complete benchmark covers 360 scenarios including three relic-capacity policies and 1,054 generated inventories.

The 60% figure is treated as a ceiling for the main tree plus the equipped companion and Afterglow paths. It is not a completion target. At 6–8 paid upgrades plus one free root, a run cannot also complete 60% of 68–70 accessible node definitions. Observed maximum completion was about 16.2%, so players retain many different next-run paths. No artificial skill completion was added to force those incompatible targets together.

Each of seven legal eight-purchase plans spends 745–785 Embers and has a functional build. Examples: Light gains Glow Sense, Echo Sight, Dawnburst, wider circular light and a six-second Dream; Motion gains the full descent guide and lighter effort/fall; Glow gains a larger vessel, Kindled Touch, Afterglow and stronger recovery; Collector gains roughly 59% reach and richer fetched fuel; Protector recharges in 10.08s instead of 14s and gets a longer deflection window; Runner recharges in 3.315s instead of 7.5s with a shorter burst; Space Timer holds for 3.798s instead of 2.4s with 65% steadiness. These signatures demonstrate effects and reachable prerequisites, not equal human difficulty. The separate delayed-input flight stress sample flags Runner/Space Timer timing sensitivity; it is too small and crude to justify universal win-rate claims.

Artifacts have COMMON, RARE and BOSS tiers. Ordinary/chest/elite pools exclude BOSS relics. Map 1/2 milestone exits guarantee two RARE run offers; they are not bosses. The final actual boss grants a chosen unowned BOSS relic permanently. Three boss bundles combine the same bounded runtime effects: Sunheart Engine links rich fuel, overflow recharge and forward light; Dream Sovereign links Dream recharge, waking reveal and free Lift in temporary light; Hollow Covenant links efficient Lift, companion work and Dark Seed skimming. Shared proc caps prevent overlapping relics from multiplying their trigger limits. Catalog source metadata lets the runtime highlight the actual equipped relic that supplied an effect.

Artifact capacity is 3 at Ascension 1, 2 at Ascensions 2–3 and 1 at Ascensions 4–6. Three independent Reliquary skills add one slot each for 140, 280 and 560 Embers, up to six at Ascension 1. They do not require Companion Bond and cannot be taken as the free root. TEST grants no free sockets. Model reward selection, equipping, modifier aggregation and save cleanup enforce capacity and prevent duplicate effects.

Saving for sockets has a measured opportunity cost. The Ascension 1 socket-focused policy spends a median 140 on one extra slot and ends with four slots. The Ascension 3/6 policies save 420 for two slots and buy six nodes total instead of eight, leaving about four ordinary ability purchases. They finish with median capacities 4/3 and median equipped counts 3/2: a bought slot is not automatically filled. The final 560-Ember socket remains an expensive specialized choice, not an expected purchase every run.

Workshop refunds use a run-owned actual-paid ledger: shrine discounts cannot be converted into full-price refunds, and the free root refunds zero. TEST Archive trades only mutate the current run preview and never the permanent collection. Practice's first victory now uses the real two-offer/cash checkpoint flow with isolated practice currency. Natural-pickup Afterglow conversion uses the runtime's accepted surplus, excluding wasted overflow from stored totals.
