# Exit packing · 15 November 2026

At a successful exit, unused flight fuel converts into Stored Sparks at the player's existing Storage rate (initially six Sparks to one Stored Spark). Fractional progress carries forward. This conversion consumes the remaining flight reserve and is separate from the capped pickup-overflow harvest. The vessel then recovers 50% of capacity, improved by Renewal and conditional recovery skills. Rest still refills without packing.

The flight previews the exact conversion in its existing packing rail. Settlement recomputes it from remaining fuel rather than trusting a quoted currency award. Saving, repeated settlement, failed attempts, and Rest cannot repeat the exit payout. Practice earnings stay in practice. Clear receipts include exit packing and pact rewards.

This release intentionally excludes the experimental supply normalizer and later-chapter obstacle pressure. The goal of requiring approximately 60% fuel capture on starter maps remains unfinished: the experimental numerical budgets passed inventory checks but failed the delayed, visibility-limited flight cohort. Those values need authored placement work and broader progression/build validation before publication.

Validation: 24 opening-flight physics scenarios retain 17 clears, matching the prior public build. A separate 48-campaign economic scenario pass reconciles banking and spending across 48 authored course variants; it does not simulate geometric navigation or establish human difficulty. All 50 focused transaction, checkpoint, reward, Collector, skill-book and engine-animation tests pass.
