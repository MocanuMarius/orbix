# Steady giants and ceiling warnings

Implementation batch, 15 November 2026.

## Trajectory Guide

The free-fall guide remains. A hypothetical Lift no longer draws a blue ascent
line. If that affordable Lift would hit overhead solid terrain within the rank's
forecast horizon, a small red pixel dot marks the actual surface contact.
Open sky, a safe Lift and unavailable fuel do not produce a ceiling warning.
The marker respects local Luminance and stops at unresolved dynamic scenery.
The upper rank's optional gold route to a visible Spark remains.

The three base prices fall from 45/65/110 to 25/40/70 Stored Sparks. Normal
run-wide training growth and prerequisite discounts still determine the quote.
The skill book describes the changed effect and uses these production prices.

## Giant Black Holes

A rare steady variant hangs beyond the roof or floor of a checked crossing,
with roughly a third of its black core visible. At the reference flight height,
its core radius is 104 versus the ordinary 16, and its drain reaches 368 versus
220. Its softened inverse-square drain is stronger near the core and lower
along the authored far lane. These encounters retain the existing fuel-drain
model; they add no movement force or new control.

The broad accretion disk and approach cue work with an offscreen centre.
Stolen light spirals into the rim, with a bounded particle budget. A paused
first-encounter lesson and the Field Guide explain which side to use.
`black-holes.html` provides a real-flight Roof/Floor rehearsal without a save.

## Verification limits

The trajectory probe compares actual physics, moving terrain, affordable Lifts
and open sky. Both game renderers were inspected at 915 × 412. The skill book,
pricing, onboarding, tutorial and live-upgrade regression checks pass.
Across 192 generated flights over 48 courses, seven natural giants passed
placement checks. The probe made 178,430 checks with the production collider.
Four actual-physics crossings used decisions every 150ms, with four Lifts each.
The far route lost 0.56–1.94 displayed Sparks in landscape. A deliberately slow
close pass lost 18.24. Giants require existing approach and recovery Sparks;
unsupported crossings retain an ordinary hole.
These placement and crossing checks are separate from human play difficulty.
Desktop browser CPU measurements do not establish performance on a Pixel 10.
