# Sim courses: what the pilots fly, and links to fly it yourself

Generated by `scripts/balance/sim-courses.mjs`. Flight seeds and route are identical to the pilots of `scripts/balance/starter-balance.mjs`; the flight is the app's own flight (`tests/sim-matches-app.test.mjs`). A stop = one flight of Map 1 on the pilots' route; Lift Guide mounted, nothing else; start Meter and hearts = the pilots' typical state at that stop (`SIM_START`: Meter 30/15/30/30/15/15 Sparks, hearts 3/3/3/3/3/2).

**Supplied** = Sparks lying in the course. **Hover needs** = Sparks an efficient hover costs for the whole duration (one Lift every 1.367 s, 3 Sparks each); the starting Meter covers the first 15 to 30. Pilot columns = share of 12 single attempts that fell, with the most common cause (fuel = the Meter ran out; obstacle / terrain / boundary = a hit). **W** marks the 6 worst stops (highest mean failure over the three pilots).
