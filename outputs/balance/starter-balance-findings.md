## Lift facts
- A Lift costs 3 Sparks (10 Meter units; 1 Spark = 3.33 units; start Meter 100 units = 30 Sparks) and always sets vy = -205 (gravity 300, terminal fall 245), whenever pressed. No wingbeat or rapid-Lift penalty in the physics. The 0.75 s / falling rule only decides whether the card trigger "Lift" fires (else "plain Lift").
- Height gained by a Lift tau seconds after the previous one = 205 t - 150 t^2: 0.1 s 19 units (6/Spark), 0.3 s 48 (16/Spark), 0.5 s 65 (22/Spark), 0.68 s 70 (23/Spark, best), 1.0 s 55, 1.367 s zero net.
- Arrow: open when fall speed is within +-0.1 s of +205 (a 0.2 s window centred 1.367 s after the last Lift, the moment the Wisp is back at its launch height); 'soon' shows 0.22 s earlier; 'late' after; needs Sparks >= cost. A Lift on the cue holds altitude for 3/1.367 = 2.19 Sparks/s (1.42 Sparks per 100 distance at 154/s). Five spam Lifts 0.2 s apart cost 15 Sparks for about 35 units each and fly to the ceiling; sustained spam at 0.3 s costs 10 Sparks/s, 4.6x the hover rate.
- Course supply (flight 1 of Map 1: 54 s, 7606 distance): 120 Sparks (1.58 per 100 distance) against 105 needed by 35 Lifts; the designer assumes 60% capture plus the 30-Spark start Meter, so required capture is 62.5%. Flight 2: 124 s, 348 Sparks supplied (1.89 per 100), 80 Lifts = 240 needed. Capture needed at hover cadence is about 60-75%: thin by design, and later flights start with a carried-over Meter that is often half (15 Sparks), where the first supply arrives after 2.7-9 s.

## Why the old pilots failed (opening flights, Lift Guide mounted)
They do not spam: 2-3% of Lifts under 0.75 s, 0% under 0.5 s. They hover non-stop (about 0.72 Lifts/s, 98% of the Sparks a non-stop hover costs) and take 83-86% of the Sparks they see. Deaths are mostly Sparks running out on flights that start at 15-17 Sparks, plus obstacle hits.

## Realistic pilots: result and an honest limit
The new pilots follow the cue by timing (on-cue 71-74% of Lifts with the arrow, spam 5-6%, a beginner wastes about 6% of Lifts), remember Spark positions on a retried stop, rest under 88% Meter, and do the card-aware behaviours listed in the report. Result (Lift Guide alone, first 6 stops, 30 runs): player-good 14/30 died, average 13/30, beginner 23/30. That is better than the targets for beginner (23% clear) only; good (53% clear vs the aim of 85%) and average (57% vs 65%) are NOT reached. Control: a pilot that sees the whole course and has no human limits (style `fuel`) died in 7 of 8 runs on the same stops (mostly obstacle hits, 0 refused Lifts); the Lift-spacing sweep showed Lifts per second stay at about 0.72 for every pilot variant I tried (taller glide band, costlier Lifts, greedier), so the corridor terrain forces a near non-stop hover and the opening stops leave little slack. So the gap to the targets is a finding about the courses and the sim's obstacle avoidance (the human planner reads terrain on a 12-unit raster with a 1.4-1.8 s horizon), not spam, and it was not hidden by cheating the pilot. Caveat on the card-aware rows: the Skim Line and Meter Mark behaviours as built (hug terrain; collect at a full Meter) cost survival for good pilots, so their aware numbers are worse than the card-blind ones; read both.

## Proposals (not implemented; coin-toss Lift Guide weighted worth T = 59.8; band 0.8-1.25x = 47.8-74.8)
Simulated rows are in report section 3; "est." rows extrapolate the Stored part, which is linear.
- Courier Cord: today -0.04x. A Delivery happens about 1.5 times per flight whatever the pilot does (the Collector's cooldown, steering does not change it: 1.63 -> 1.55 per flight card-aware). Simulated: "each Delivery +6 Sparks and +5 Stored" = 43.4 (0.72x). Proposal: **each Delivery adds 6 Sparks to the Meter and 6 Stored Sparks**, est. 52 (0.87x).
- Finish Flag: today 0.10x. Simulated: "+6 Stored and the Meter refilled to full at the next flight" = 39.9 (0.67x). Proposal: **Finish adds 8 Stored Sparks and refills the Meter to full for the next flight**, est. 52 (0.87x). (Fuel is what pays: +4 Stored with refill 10 is 0.33x, with refill 20 is 0.55x.)
- Skim Line: today negative with an aware pilot (risk of hugging terrain). Skims run about 1/flight blind, 1.5-1.9 aware. Simulated "+6 Sparks and +4 Stored per Skim" = 18 (0.30x). A Skim is too rare to carry it: **widen the skim clearance from 48 to 64 and pay 6 Sparks and 8 Stored per Skim**, est. 40-50 (0.7-0.85x, uncertain: risk cost not modelled for the wider band).
- Meter Mark: 0.17x (aware, noisy) / 0.46x (card-blind, Lift Guide's today-world); about 10 Overfills per flight: **each Overfill keeps 2 Stored Sparks** (was 1), est. 1.0-1.2x of T.
- Pulse Reader: 0.57x (old pilots 0.76x): **its own Pulse every 5 s instead of 8**, est. 0.9x.
