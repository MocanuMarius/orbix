## Findings

- 1 Spark is worth about 0.1 Stored at the end of a flight (Lift Guide: 51 Sparks -> 5.9; Pulse Reader: 20 -> 1.2-2.5). Leftover Sparks pack at about 1/6 and the pilot never ran short, so extra Sparks enabled no extra Lifts (extra Lifts about 0). Direct Stored is worth 1:1. So every card that pays Stored is worth roughly 6-10x a card that pays the same number of Sparks.
- Meter Mark: 9.4 Overfills per flight alone, 17.2 beside Lift Guide (Lift Guide's Sparks keep the Meter full), 34 with Echo + Lift Guide. Each pays 1 Stored: 25-45% of a cleared flight's 37 Stored.
- Finish Flag +2 is an exact, reliable anchor. Echo doubles it (4), as it does every card.
- Pulse Reader fires about 19-21 times per flight on every map: it works without help but is worth little because the Sparks overfill the Meter.
- Pays almost nothing in a typical flight: Courier Cord (1.4-1.9 Deliveries, 0.1-0.3 Stored equiv.) and Skim Line (0.9-1.8 Skims, about 0 Stored equiv.). With Echo they double but stay under 1.
- Lift Guide: on the cue for 67% of Lifts with the pilot (it does not watch the arrow; 98% of Lifts are falling Lifts). Assumption for a human who has learned the cue: 70-90%, so 6-8 Stored equiv. Its worth is mostly fuel (a Lift costs 3, so an on-cue Lift is refunded one third), which the Stored measure cannot see.

## Proposed smallest changes (identity and teaching cue unchanged)

1. Meter Mark: keep 1 spilled Spark as Stored -> keep 1 Stored per 8 Overfills (a count on the card, like the packing remainder; no cap). Expected 1.2 alone, 2.2 beside Lift Guide, 4.3 with Echo + Lift Guide. Every 6th gives 1.6 / 2.9. Needs a remainder carried between flights or per flight; ask the owner whether "a fraction of a Stored Spark per Overfill" (0.125) is acceptable wording.
2. Courier Cord: Delivery +1 Spark -> +1 Stored. Expected 1.4-1.9.
3. Skim Line: Skim +1 Spark -> +2 Stored (a Skim needs a held band and happens about once per flight). Expected 1.8-2.4.
4. Lift Guide: leave at +1 Spark. It is the shared baseline every start has, and +1 Spark per on-cue Lift is the smallest unit; lowering it would break its fuel-teaching role. Re-check the three others against 1.6 after changes 1-3 (median becomes about 2).
5. Pulse Reader and Finish Flag: no change.
