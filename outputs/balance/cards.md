## Firing model (assumption)

One flight is 30 seconds. Firings per flight: Collect 10, Delivery 4, Latch 3, Pulse 6, Flash 3, Flash ends 3, Falling Lift 8, Overfill 2, Rhythm 4, Save 1, Clear 1. No card listens to: Swipe, Victory.
Each firing uses a fixed repeating mix of pickups (Collect: 6 plain Sparks, 2 Clusters, 1 Cache, 1 Dusk Spark; meter low on 3 of 10), so conditional cards get a plain share. Limited-Use cards run down across the flight like the engine. Chains hold distinct cards, rank 1, all pairs and all ordered triples of cards that can act on the Trigger. Values are Sparks per flight (each firing rounded down) with every firing capped at 100, the Spark Meter's size; a chain that reaches 100 on every firing is marked saturated.
