## Fixed after the first audit (2026-11-18)

- Ring Spring, Ring Spring II: on an Overfill they now bank their seconds and take them off the NEXT Collector recovery (like Haul Winch); the banked seconds show on the card badge while they wait.
- Crate Lining: also pays on a Cluster delivery, at half (rounded up).
- Pulse Reader: sends its own Pulse every 8 s, so it works alone.

## Probed inside the flight (scripts/balance/card-works-probes.mjs)

- Heart Stitch (id tailwind), Springboard, Long Spool, Cast Line, Black Hole and Devourer each have a probe that measures the physical effect against the printed text.

## Design problems found by the probes (one proposal each, nothing redesigned)

- Springboard: the face and the help say "half-Lift"; the bounce is half a Lift's launch SPEED, which rises only a quarter of a Lift's height. Proposal: launch at 0.71 of a Lift (half the height), or word it "a small bounce" and keep the numbers.
- Heart Stitch keeps the id 'tailwind' (the "+6% pace" Tailwind is the Skill main-tailwind, a different thing). Proposal: rename the id to heart-stitch the next time saves are migrated, so audits and docs stop confusing the two.

## Not flown here

- Last Light is proven by the solver only (last heart: 2 -> 4 Sparks), not in a flight.
- Rest cards (Second Ember, Growth Seeds) are covered by tests/card-camp-progression.test.mjs.
- Finish cards (Finish Flag, Home Beacon, Growth Ring, Wind Feather, Wayfarer's Purse, Nest Egg) are proven through the model's finishFlight receipt, not WispFlight (a Finish is resolved there).
