## Designs that do not work in real flights (no redesign done; one proposal each)

- Ring Spring, Ring Spring II (Overfill: Collector ready sooner). The Overfill reached the Chain 12 times in 2 flights but it never fired: it needs a Collector that is still recovering at that moment. Proposal: bank the seconds like Haul Winch (shorten the NEXT recovery) so an Overfill always pays.
- Crate Lining (Delivery of a Cache: +8). Deliveries happen (5 in 2 flights) but a Cache is almost never the delivered pickup. Proposal: also pay on a Cluster delivery at half, or make the Collector prefer Caches.
- Not provable by this audit (they act inside the flight, no run modifier, no flight probe yet): Tailwind (Heart Stitch), Springboard, Long Spool, Cast Line, Black Hole, Devourer. Proposal: one real-flight probe each (Springboard: free bounce per pickup; Spools: a catch beyond normal reach spends a use; Black Hole/Devourer: take-off / clear receipts).
- Last Light is proven by the solver only (last heart: 2 -> 4 Sparks), not in a flight.
- Rest cards (Second Ember, Growth Seeds) are covered by tests/card-camp-progression.test.mjs, not flown here.
- Finish cards (Finish Flag, Home Beacon, Growth Ring, Wind Feather, Wayfarer's Purse, Nest Egg) are proven through the model's finishFlight receipt, not WispFlight (a Finish is resolved there).
